# YouTube Description SEO

A plain-language reference on writing YouTube video descriptions and companion content that get found, by YouTube search, by Google, and by AI answer engines. It covers the description structure, the companion blog post, transcripts and chapters, and the citability rules that make video content quotable. Written for creators who want their videos to be discovered and cited.

An independent project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.

## The core idea

A YouTube description does double duty: it helps viewers and it feeds search and AI engines that decide whether to surface and cite your video. The description should lead with a clear, specific summary of what the video covers, in the first lines, then provide structure, links, and context. Pairing each video with a companion post that carries the full transcript, summary, and chapters multiplies where the content can be found and quoted.
