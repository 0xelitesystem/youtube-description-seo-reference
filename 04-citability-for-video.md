# Citability for video

Make video content citable by writing the companion post and description so that key points are stated plainly, in self-contained passages, in the words people search. AI engines quote the text around your video, so the text has to answer questions directly, the same way a citable web page does.

## Engines quote the text, not the footage

When an AI engine surfaces or cites video content, it draws on the text it can read: the description, the transcript, and the companion post, not the footage itself. So citability for video is really citability for the text that accompanies it. Writing that text to be quotable, with direct answers and clear structure, is what gives the video a path into AI citations. The footage matters to viewers; the text matters to engines.

## Lead with the answer

In the companion post and the description summary, state the key points directly and early, rather than burying them in narrative. A passage that answers a question plainly, in its first sentence, is what an engine can lift and cite. Video content often rambles or builds up to its points; the accompanying text should distill those points into direct, quotable statements. The text is the chance to make the video's substance citable even if the video itself meanders.

## Self-contained takeaways

Write the key takeaways as self-contained statements that make sense on their own, without needing the rest of the video for context. An engine extracting a takeaway lifts it away from everything around it, so it has to stand alone. Phrasing each main point as a complete, standalone statement, with its own subject named, is what lets it survive extraction. A list of self-contained takeaways is among the most citable parts of a companion post.

## Use the question language

Phrase chapter titles, takeaways, and headings as the questions people actually ask, then answer them directly in the text beneath. When the question is on the page in the words a person would search, and the answer follows immediately, the match between a query and your content is clear to the engine. This is the same direct-answer pattern that makes web pages citable, applied to the text wrapped around a video. Question, then immediate answer, repeated through the companion post.
