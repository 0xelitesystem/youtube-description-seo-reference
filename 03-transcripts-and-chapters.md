# Transcripts and chapters

A full transcript and time-stamped chapters are the parts of video content that search and AI engines extract from most readily. The transcript turns spoken content into indexable, quotable text, and chapters give both viewers and engines a structured map of what the video covers.

## The transcript is the content in text

A transcript renders everything said in the video as text, which is the form engines can index and quote. Without a transcript, the substance of a video is locked in audio that engines handle poorly; with one, every point made becomes searchable and citable. Including the full transcript, on the companion post and where the platform supports captions, is the single biggest step toward making video content discoverable as text.

## Clean the transcript where it counts

A raw auto-generated transcript captures the words but often lacks punctuation and structure, which makes it harder to read and to extract from cleanly. Lightly editing the transcript into readable paragraphs, fixing obvious errors and adding structure, improves both the reader's experience and how well engines can pull clean passages from it. The transcript does not need to be perfect prose, but readable beats raw for extraction.

## Chapters as a map

Time-stamped chapters break the video into labeled sections, which helps viewers jump to what they want and signals the video's structure to discovery systems. Chapter titles, phrased as the topics or questions each section addresses, act like headings, telling both viewers and engines what the video covers and where. Good chapters turn a long video into a navigable, parseable structure rather than an undifferentiated stretch.

## Structure mirrors citability

The combination of a clean transcript and clear chapters gives video content the same qualities that make a web page citable: extractable text organized into labeled, self-contained sections. A chapter with a clear title and a transcript passage that answers it directly is the video equivalent of a heading and a direct-answer paragraph. Structuring video content this way is applying the citability approach to a medium that otherwise hides its content from engines.
