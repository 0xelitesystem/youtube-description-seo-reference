# The checklist

A per-video check for description and companion content that gets found and cited.

## Description

- [ ] The first lines summarize what the video covers, specifically, in search language.
- [ ] Topics covered are expanded briefly after the opening.
- [ ] Time-stamped chapters are included.
- [ ] Links and calls to action come after the summary, not before.
- [ ] A link to the companion post is present.

## Companion post

- [ ] A post on your own site embeds the video.
- [ ] The post includes a written summary and key takeaways.
- [ ] The full transcript is included and lightly cleaned for readability.
- [ ] Time-stamped chapters are listed.
- [ ] The post and video link to each other, including a pinned comment.

## Citability

- [ ] Key points are stated directly and early, not buried in narrative.
- [ ] Takeaways are self-contained and make sense on their own.
- [ ] Chapter titles and headings are phrased as the questions people ask.
- [ ] The text uses the real words people would search.

## The one-line version

Lead the description with a specific summary in search language, pair every video with a companion post carrying the transcript, summary, takeaways, and chapters, and write that text to answer questions directly. The video reaches viewers; the text reaches search and AI engines.
